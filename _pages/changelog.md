# Changelog

### `Initial Release`
# **Version 1.0**

Welcome to **Alcatraz**! It's 1962, and you have three days to get ready and one night to escape.

#### Features

- 🏝️ **Eight Scenes** — The cell, the kitchen, the library, the laundry, the warden's office, the utility corridor, the old fort's dungeons and the dock
- 🌙 **Three Days, Then One Night** — Gather what your escape plan needs by day, then break out in a single night
- 🧩 **Puzzles With Rules to Figure Out** — A dumbwaiter, a relief map, a laundry chute, a clock's movement, a pontoon in the dark
- 🗝️ **Characters Who Guide You** — The Old Man next door, the guards and the warden
- 💡 **One Hint at a Time** — Matched to where you are, never the answer
- 🔓 **First Two Scenes Free** — Unlock the full game with a single purchase
